---
name: arxiv
description: Use arXiv as a domain-specific source for computing, mathematics, physics and computational research.
---

Use the registered literature-search tool when arXiv is relevant to the research question. Record the actual translated query and avoid silently dropping Boolean exclusions.

Preserve arXiv identifiers, versions, dates and publication metadata. Withdrawn records are not supporting studies. A preprint and its published version are not two independent studies merely because their identifiers differ.

Read the abstract before selecting a body for acquisition. Use the corresponding original version for numerical or methodological claims; do not claim to have read a PDF because its link is present. Report source-access failures through the host's auditable result instead of fabricating a paper or bypassing access restrictions.
