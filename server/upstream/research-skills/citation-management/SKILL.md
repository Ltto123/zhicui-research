---
name: citation-management
description: Audit source identity, citation coverage and claim support using saved original evidence.
---

Treat source identity, access, reading coverage and semantic support as separate checks.

Use the current saved draft and host citation audit. Count only independently identified works actually cited in the body; do not confuse the complete candidate corpus with the cited subset. User materials and computed records do not become external papers.

Resolve ambiguous DOI/title/version identities with the registered evidence tools. Do not fill missing authors, journals, years, volume or page numbers by guessing. Preserve the source ID while editing prose. Bibliography formatting and source links are generated by the host.

For each disputed claim, inspect the paired original passage and its context. Keep qualifiers, populations and comparison directions. Notes that omit a detail do not establish that the original paper omitted it. Correct unsupported precision or causal language without inventing replacement evidence.

Review warnings are not facts. Recheck the original passage and the affected manuscript paragraph. Correct its claim or withdraw a mistaken criticism. Keep the workbench's acquisition and review statistics in the user's run record. Missing minimum citations still block delivery, but irrelevant references must never be added just to satisfy a number.

Use the host's `citationReadiness` / `eligibleForWriting` for the current manuscript. A candidate may exist and be visible through `read_saved` while still requiring full-text acquisition or verified reading. Reading its saved abstract does not upgrade that status. Distinguish a nonexistent ID from an existing but ineligible source; never guess a replacement ID. Correct only the affected claim or obtain the required evidence, then let the host check the manuscript automatically.

Already resolved issues and explicit “no change needed” findings belong in checks, not blocking errors. Retain mixed findings that still require a concrete correction. Do not resend unchanged work for review or ask the user to resolve internal citation, synchronization or formatting checks.
